How machines work

Components take up space in your machine's grid. Each has sockets (the circles) that want a certain element: a matching rune powers it fully, a different element only 40%, and higher-rank runes are worth more (rank II = 2×, III = 4×…). Many components boost the components they touch, so placement matters. Every component from the Rift is rolled: a rarity (Common → Legendary), its own socket elements, and random modifiers. Every kill in the Rift drops Essence ✦, and you can salvage spare runes and components for more. Spend Essence to upgrade components and expand your grid. Resonance is your machine's total power: it unlocks harder Rift Tiers. If your machine is destroyed in the Rift, it keeps half of what it found, and each installed component (and socketed rune) has a 30% chance to be lost.
