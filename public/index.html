How machines work

Components take up space in your machine's grid. Each has sockets that want a certain element: a matching rune powers it fully, a different element only 40%, and higher-rank runes are worth more (rank II = 2×, III = 4×…). Many components boost the components they touch, so placement matters: click any component to see what it gives and gets. Every component from the Rift is rolled: a rarity, a random shape and sockets, buffs and sometimes curses. The runes socketed together in one component make element combos. Every kill in the Rift drops Essence; salvage spares for more, and spend it to upgrade components and expand your chassis. Resonance is your machine's total power and unlocks harder Rift Tiers. If your machine is destroyed, it keeps half of what it found, and each installed component (and socketed rune) has a 30% chance to be lost.
